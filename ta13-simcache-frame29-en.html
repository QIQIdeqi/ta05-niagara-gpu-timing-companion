Why does sequence frame 29 map to source step 31?

This test aligned sequence frame 0 with cache index 0. Recording started at source step 2, so the recorded source step is the cache index plus 2 for these samples. In your own project, check the section start and the actual recording start before applying any offset.

Compare the three samples

  1. Download the 96-row CSV. Split the rows by condition: A (original recording), CachePlayback, and IndependentRecompute.
  2. Keep only x_cm, y_cm, and z_cm. Sort each group numerically by X, then Y, then Z. Compare the sorted rows and count matches. Keep duplicate coordinates.
  3. Both component logs report an age of about 0.516667 seconds at this point. The playback component had advanced twice, while the independent recomputation had advanced 31 times. What do the coordinates, age, and advance counts each tell you?
Check your answer

All 32 coordinate triples match across the three groups at CSV precision, including duplicates. The playback probe requested ages at three recorded points and advanced synchronously. Step 31 was its second request. The independent component simulated step by step from its starting point. The advance logs and cache attachment state distinguish the two paths.

Check your cache track in Unreal Engine

  1. After building the track, open Sequencer from the Level Sequence Actor in the level. Check the target Niagara component binding and the Sim Cache assigned to the section. Set the playback policy to Display Cache Only.
  2. For this example, scrub 0 → 29 → 57 → 70 → 0. Expect particles at the first three points, no particles at frame 70 outside the section, and particles again on returning to frame 0. For your own recording, pick three frames inside the section and one outside it.
  3. Save the level, sequence and cache. Close and reopen the same level, then repeat the check from its Level Sequence Actor. If the section is blank, verify the binding and cache assignment first, then check the viewport framing and whether the recording contains particles.

Record the Unreal version, section range, four check points, and results before and after reopening. Use sampled data to verify XYZ matches.

What this test establishes

These coordinates represent one recorded time in one trial. The comparison checks positions without tracking particle identity. The capture contains no GPU timing measurements. The CSV extracts the selected time and coordinate columns from three original records. AI assisted with page editing and translation.
