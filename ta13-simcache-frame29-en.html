Niagara Sim Cache playback: same coordinates, different update paths

In UE 5.8.4, the test used one CPU emitter with a fixed seed and timestep. The sequence ran at 60 fps. Recording started at source simulation step 2, so zero-based cache frame 29 corresponds to source simulation step 31.

Compare the three samples

  1. Download the 96-row CSV. Split the rows by condition: A (original recording), CachePlayback, and IndependentRecompute.
  2. Keep only x_cm, y_cm, and z_cm. Sort each group numerically by X, then Y, then Z. Compare the sorted rows and count matches. Keep duplicate coordinates.
  3. Both component logs report an age of about 0.516667 seconds at this point. The playback component had advanced twice, while the independent recomputation had advanced 31 times. What do the coordinates, age, and advance counts each tell you?
Check your answer

All 32 coordinate triples match across the three groups at CSV precision, including duplicates. The playback probe requested ages at three recorded points and advanced synchronously. Step 31 was its second request. The independent component simulated step by step from its starting point. The advance logs and cache attachment state distinguish the two paths.

What this test establishes

These coordinates represent one recorded time in one trial. The comparison checks positions without tracking particle identity. The capture contains no GPU timing measurements. The CSV extracts the selected time and coordinate columns from three original records. AI assisted with page editing and translation.
