Two Niagara states inside one MRQ output frame

In this UE 5.8.4 / D3D11 test, Movie Render Queue takes two temporal samples for each output frame. The first and second samples at frame 56 report different states for both short cache sections. Both components match within each condition, with cache assets A and B bound to separate components.

Follow the state from frames 54 to 57

The two Niagara Actors reported the same cache binding and execution state in the reversed run. C0 was bound and Active at all eight callbacks. From frame 54 at 0/2 through frame 56 at 0/2, both short sections were unbound: C1 reported Complete and C1Live reported Active. From frame 56 at 1/2 through frame 57 at 1/2, both were bound and Active.

These observations bracket the state change between the two frame 56 callbacks. The state readings agree at frame 57, while the final images still need a separate comparison.

Test conditions and limits

60 fps, 240 ticks/s, centered shutter, motion blur 0.5, and two temporal samples. All 48 state records matched across the two job orders. With the probe on and off in the reversed run, all 12 G>30 foreground mask pairs matched. The largest RGB difference in any channel was one 8-bit level. The contribution of each sample to the final image and the source of those small RGB differences remain open.
