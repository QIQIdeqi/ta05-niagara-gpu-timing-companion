Why does one Niagara cache track return to the first frame?

This UE 5.8.4 test used one CPU emitter. Both Sequencer sections use a 60 fps display rate, Repeat playback and the display-frame range [0,59). The internal tick resolutions are 24,000 and 60 ticks per second. A disables interpolation and B enables it.
