R001 · A review note you can verify

Reference: SH010 v001, 24 fps, non-drop-frame timecode, zero-based frames. Review from 00:00:02:00 inclusive to 00:00:03:00 exclusive. Keep the character still with its right edge short of the red line. The artist delivered SH010 v002. The reviewer has yet to confirm it.

Use these single-frame measurements at 00:00:02:12. Both versions share the same horizontal coordinates. The red line is at x=460, and the right edge of the character is at x=492 in v001 and x=440 in v002. Decide what this frame shows and what evidence would confirm that the character holds still.

Check R001

SH010 v001 → v002. Review frames 48–71. Frame 72 is the exclusive endpoint. Mark the note “awaiting review” and assign the next check to the reviewer. 00:00:02:12 is frame 60. The character’s right edge is at x=492 in v001 and x=440 in v002. The red line is at x=460. Check crossing on frame 60 and check the whole range to verify that the character holds still.
